Congruent & Similar Figures

The Question

Two rectangles are similar. The smaller one has sides 4 and 6. The larger one has corresponding sides 12 and x. What is x?

The Idea

Congruent figures are identical in size and shape. Similar figures have the same shape but a different size, related by a scale factor — every side of one figure is the same multiple of the corresponding side of the other.

In one line: find the scale factor from one known pair of corresponding sides, then apply it to the missing side.

Watch It Solved

known pair: 4 → 12

scale factor = 12 ÷ 4 = 3

x = 6 × 3

x = 18

Watch Out

A common mistake is matching up sides that aren't actually corresponding (e.g. pairing the longer side of one figure with the shorter side of the other) — always match sides in the same position/order as the figures are labeled.

Why It Matters

Similar figures are behind scale models, maps, blueprints, and photo resizing — anywhere a shape needs to grow or shrink while keeping its proportions exactly the same.
