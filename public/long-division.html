➗Long Division

The Question

You have 936 candies to pack equally into 4 boxes. How many candies go in each box?

The Idea

Long division breaks a big division into small steps: divide, multiply, subtract, then bring down the next digit — and repeat.

In one line: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — repeat for each digit.

Watch It Solved

936 ÷ 4

9 ÷ 4 = 2 remainder 1 → write 2, bring down the next digit → 13

13 ÷ 4 = 3 remainder 1 → write 3, bring down the next digit → 16

16 ÷ 4 = 4 remainder 0

936 ÷ 4 = 234

Watch Out

A common mistake is forgetting to bring down the next digit before dividing again — that skips a digit in the answer. Always bring down exactly one digit at a time.

Why It Matters

Long division is how you split any large amount evenly without a calculator — money, supplies, teams.
