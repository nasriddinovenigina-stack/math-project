🌳Prime Factorization

The Question

You want to split 60 stickers into equal groups, but you're not sure which group sizes work. Breaking 60 into its prime building blocks shows every possible way.

The Idea

Prime factorization means writing a number as a product of only prime numbers (2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ...). Keep dividing by the smallest prime that fits until only primes are left.

In one line: keep dividing by the smallest prime that fits, until only primes remain.

Watch It Solved

60

60 ÷ 2 = 30

30 ÷ 2 = 15

15 ÷ 3 = 5

60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5

Watch Out

A common mistake is stopping too early and leaving a non-prime factor, like writing 60 = 2 × 2 × 15. Keep breaking down every factor until each one is prime.

Why It Matters

Prime factorization is the foundation for finding the GCF and LCM of numbers, and for simplifying fractions.
