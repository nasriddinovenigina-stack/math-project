📦Volume of Solids

The Question

A fish tank is shaped like a rectangular box: 40 cm long, 20 cm wide, and 25 cm tall. How much water (in cubic cm) does it hold when full?

The Idea

Volume measures how much space is inside a 3D shape. For a rectangular box (prism), multiply length × width × height. For a cube, all three sides are equal, so it's just side × side × side.

In one line: volume of a box = length × width × height.

Watch It Solved

length = 40, width = 20, height = 25

V = 40 × 20 × 25

V = 800 × 25

V = 20,000 cm³

Watch Out

A common mistake is forgetting the units — volume is always in CUBIC units (like cm³ or m³), not plain units, because you're filling a 3D space, not measuring a line or a flat area. If your answer doesn't have a "³", double check.

Why It Matters

Volume tells you how much a container holds — water tanks, boxes, rooms, shipping containers — anywhere you need to know "how much fits inside."
