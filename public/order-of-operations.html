Order of Operations

You have 2,000 so'm saved, plus you work 3 shifts earning 4,000 so'm each. How much do you have in total?

When a problem has more than one operation, do things in this order: parentheses first, then multiplication, then addition/subtraction.

In one line: multiply (and parentheses) before you add or subtract.

2 + 3 × 4

multiply first → 3 × 4 = 12

2 + 12

= 14

A common mistake is working strictly left to right and getting 2 + 3 = 5, then 5 × 4 = 20 — the wrong order. Multiplication always happens before addition, no matter which comes first on the page.

Without an agreed order, the same expression could mean different things to different people — this rule keeps math consistent for everyone.
