Arithmetic: Addition, Subtraction & Multiplication

Arithmetic is the foundation of math: combining numbers using +, −, and ×.

  • Addition (a + b): combine two amounts into one total.
  • Subtraction (a − b): find the difference, or what's left after taking b away from a.
  • Multiplication (a × b): repeated addition of a, b times.

Example: 7 + 5 = 12, 9 − 4 = 5, 6 × 3 = 18.

Fractions: Adding Fractions

A fraction like a/b means a parts out of b equal parts. To add two fractions, first give them a common denominator, then add the numerators.

Example: 1/4 + 1/6 → common denominator 12 → 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12.

Always simplify your answer to lowest terms if possible (e.g. 2/4 simplifies to 1/2).

Enter answers as a fraction, like 3/4, or a whole number if it simplifies to one.

Basic Algebra: Solving for x

An equation like ax + b = c says "some number x, multiplied by a and then increased by b, equals c." To solve for x, undo the operations in reverse order: first subtract b from both sides, then divide by a.

Example: 2x + 3 = 11 → subtract 3: 2x = 8 → divide by 2: x = 4.
