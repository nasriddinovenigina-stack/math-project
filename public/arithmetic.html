➕Arithmetic: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication & Division

The Question

You're at the bazaar. You pick up 7 tomatoes, then grab 5 more — how many now? You started with 9 onions and used 4 in soup — how many are left? You buy 3 trays of 6 eggs each — how many eggs in total? And now you want to pack those 18 eggs equally into cartons of 6 — how many cartons do you need?

The Idea

Add to combine, subtract to find the difference, multiply to repeat-add, and divide to split into equal groups.

In one line: add to combine, subtract to take away, multiply to repeat-add, divide to split evenly.

Watch It Solved

7 + 5 = 12

9 − 4 = 5

6 × 3 = 18

18 ÷ 6 = 3

Watch Out

A common mistake is mixing up the order in subtraction or division — 9 − 4 is not the same as 4 − 9, and 18 ÷ 6 is not the same as 6 ÷ 18. Always keep the first number first.

Why It Matters
