Arithmetic: Addition, Subtraction & Multiplication

You're at the bazaar. You pick up 7 tomatoes, then grab 5 more — how many now? You started with 9 onions and used 4 in soup — how many are left? And you buy 3 trays of 6 eggs each — how many eggs in total?

Add to combine, subtract to find the difference, multiply to repeat-add.

In one line: add to combine, subtract to take away, multiply to repeat-add.

7 + 5 = 12

9 − 4 = 5

6 × 3 = 18

A common mistake is mixing up the order in subtraction — 9 − 4 is not the same as 4 − 9. Always subtract the second number from the first, not the other way around.

These three moves are the foundation for every other topic on this site — you'll use them constantly, from shopping to cooking to counting anything.
