📐Perimeter & Area

The Question

A rectangular room is 4 meters wide and 6 meters long. How much flooring (area) do you need, and how much baseboard (perimeter) goes around the walls?

The Idea

Perimeter is the distance around a shape — add up all the sides. Area is the space inside a shape — for a rectangle, multiply length × width.

In one line: perimeter = add all the sides, area = length × width (for a rectangle).

Watch It Solved

length = 6 m, width = 4 m

perimeter = add all 4 sides → 2×length + 2×width

P = 2(6) + 2(4)

P = 12 + 8

P = 20 m

area = length × width

A = 6 × 4

A = 24 m²

Watch Out

A common mistake is mixing up perimeter and area — perimeter is a single distance measured in plain units (like meters), while area is measured in square units (like m²) because it covers a 2D space. If your answer doesn't have a "²", double check you're not confusing the two.

Why It Matters

Perimeter and area show up in fencing yards, flooring rooms, framing pictures, and any time you need to measure the edge or the space of something.
