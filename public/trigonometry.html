🧭Trigonometric Ratios (SOH-CAH-TOA)

The Question

A right triangle has a side of 3 opposite one angle, a side of 4 adjacent to it, and a hypotenuse of 5. What is the sine of that angle?

The Idea

In a right triangle, SOH-CAH-TOA tells you the three basic ratios: Sine = Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cosine = Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tangent = Opposite/Adjacent.

In one line: SOH-CAH-TOA — sine, cosine, and tangent are each a ratio of two sides.

Watch It Solved

opposite = 3, adjacent = 4, hypotenuse = 5

sine = opposite ÷ hypotenuse

sin = 3/5

= 0.6

Watch Out

A common mistake is mixing up which side is opposite versus adjacent — the opposite side is across from the angle, while the adjacent side touches it (and isn't the hypotenuse). Always identify the angle first, then label the sides relative to it.

Why It Matters

Trigonometric ratios let you find unknown heights, distances, and angles without measuring them directly — used in construction, navigation, and engineering.
