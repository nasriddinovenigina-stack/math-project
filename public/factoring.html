Factoring Trinomials

The Question

Can you rewrite x² + 5x + 6 as a product of two simpler expressions, (x + ?)(x + ?)?

The Idea

To factor x² + bx + c into (x + p)(x + q), find two numbers p and q that multiply to c and add to b — this is exactly the reverse of the FOIL process.

In one line: find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b.

Watch It Solved

x² + 5x + 6

need two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 5

2 × 3 = 6, and 2 + 3 = 5

x² + 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3)

Watch Out

A common mistake is finding a pair that multiplies correctly but forgetting to check the sum too (or vice versa) — both conditions, product AND sum, must match at the same time.

Why It Matters

Factoring is how you solve quadratic equations, simplify algebraic fractions, and find the roots of a parabola without a formula — it's the reverse gear for everything FOIL builds forward.
