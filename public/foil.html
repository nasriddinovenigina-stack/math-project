✳️Multiplying Binomials (FOIL)

The Question

A rectangular garden has sides (x + 3) and (x + 5). What expression gives its area?

The Idea

To multiply two binomials, use FOIL: multiply the First terms, the Outer terms, the Inner terms, and the Last terms, then combine like terms.

In one line: First, Outer, Inner, Last — then combine like terms.

Watch It Solved

(x + 3)(x + 5)

First: x × x = x², Outer: x × 5 = 5x, Inner: 3 × x = 3x, Last: 3 × 5 = 15

x² + 5x + 3x + 15

x² + 8x + 15

Watch Out

A common mistake is only multiplying the First and Last terms (getting x² + 15) and skipping the Outer and Inner terms entirely. All four products are needed before combining like terms.

Why It Matters

Multiplying binomials is how you expand area expressions, and it's the reverse of factoring, which you'll use throughout algebra.

Enter your answer as two numbers separated by a comma: the coefficient of x, then the constant term — like 8,15 for x² + 8x + 15.
