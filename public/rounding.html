🔵Rounding Numbers

The Question

A stadium says 42,738 people attended a match. News reports usually round crowd sizes to the nearest thousand — what number would they report?

The Idea

To round to a place value, look at the digit immediately to its right. If that digit is 5 or more, round up; if it's less than 5, round down — and every digit after the rounding place becomes 0.

In one line: check the next digit — 5 or more rounds up, less than 5 rounds down.

Watch It Solved

42,738 rounded to the nearest thousand

the digit right after the thousands place is 7 → that's 5 or more, so round up

= 43,000

Watch Out

A common mistake is looking at the wrong digit — for example checking the tens digit when rounding to the nearest hundred. Always look at exactly one digit to the right of the place you're rounding to.

Why It Matters

Rounding makes big numbers easier to talk about and compare — crowd sizes, populations, prices — without needing every exact digit.
