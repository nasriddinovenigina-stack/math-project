📍Coordinate Plane: Plotting Points

The Question

A treasure map says: start at the origin, go 3 steps east and 4 steps north. Where do you mark the X?

The Idea

A point on the coordinate plane is written as (x, y). The x-value tells you how far to move left/right (horizontal), and the y-value tells you how far to move up/down (vertical), starting from the origin (0, 0).

In one line: x tells you left-right, y tells you up-down — always x first, then y.

Watch It Solved

Point: (3, 4)

start at the origin (0, 0)

move 3 steps right (positive x)

move 4 steps up (positive y)

mark the point at (3, 4)

Watch Out

A common mistake is reversing the order — plotting (4, 3) instead of (3, 4), or moving up before right. Always read the x-coordinate first, then the y-coordinate, in that exact order.

Why It Matters

Coordinates are the foundation for graphing lines, functions, and shapes — everything from maps to video game positions uses this same x, y system.
