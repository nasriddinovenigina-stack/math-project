🏷️Unit Rate

The Question

One store sells 4 kg of rice for 32,000 so'm. Another sells 6 kg for 42,000 so'm. Which store has the better price per kilogram?

The Idea

A unit rate compares an amount to just 1 unit of something — divide the total by the number of units to find the price (or amount) per one.

In one line: divide the total by the number of units to get the rate per one.

Watch It Solved

Store A: 32,000 so'm ÷ 4 kg

= 8,000 so'm per kg

Store B: 42,000 so'm ÷ 6 kg

= 7,000 so'm per kg

Store B has the lower price per kg → the better deal

Watch Out

A common mistake is comparing the totals directly (32,000 vs 42,000) instead of the price per unit — that ignores that the amounts aren't equal. Always divide down to "per one" before comparing.

Why It Matters

Unit rates are how you compare prices at the store, speeds, or any two quantities measured in different total amounts.
