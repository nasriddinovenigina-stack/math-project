🎁Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism

The Question

You're wrapping a gift box that is 4 cm tall, 3 cm wide, and 5 cm long. How much wrapping paper do you need to cover every side?

The Idea

The surface area of a rectangular prism (box) is the total area of all 6 faces. Since opposite faces match, the formula is SA = 2(lw + lh + wh).

In one line: find the area of the three different faces, add them up, then double it.

Watch It Solved

l = 5, w = 3, h = 4

lw = 5 × 3 = 15, lh = 5 × 4 = 20, wh = 3 × 4 = 12

SA = 2(15 + 20 + 12)

SA = 2 × 47 = 94 cm²

Watch Out

A common mistake is forgetting to double the sum — each of the three face areas appears twice (front/back, left/right, top/bottom), so leaving out the ×2 gives only half the true surface area.

Why It Matters

Surface area tells you how much material covers the outside of something — wrapping paper, paint, packaging.
