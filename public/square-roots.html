🌱Square Roots

The Question

You want to build a square garden with an area of 49 square meters. How long should each side be?

The Idea

A square root asks "what number, times itself, gives this?" The symbol is √.

In one line: ask what number times itself gives you this.

Watch It Solved

√49 = ?

what number times itself is 49?

7 × 7 = 49

√49 = 7

Watch Out

A common mistake is dividing by 2 instead — thinking √49 means 49 ÷ 2. A square root is about multiplication, not division: find the number that multiplies by itself to reach 49.

Why It Matters

Square roots undo squaring, and they're the tool behind the Pythagorean theorem and the quadratic formula.
