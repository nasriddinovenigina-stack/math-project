Exponents: Repeated Multiplication

A bacteria colony triples in size every hour. If it starts as 1 and triples 3 times, how big is it?

An exponent tells you how many times to multiply a number by itself.

In one line: an exponent means multiply the base by itself, that many times.

3³ = ?

multiply 3 by itself 3 times

3 × 3 × 3

= 27

A common mistake is multiplying the base by the exponent instead — thinking 3³ = 3 × 3 = 9. It actually means 3 × 3 × 3, multiplied three times, not twice.

Exponents describe explosive growth — populations, interest, viral spread — and are the basis for square roots and scientific notation.
