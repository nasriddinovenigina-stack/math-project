✖️Exponents: Repeated Multiplication

What an Exponent Means

The Question

A bacteria colony triples in size every hour. If it starts as 1 and triples 3 times, how big is it?

The Idea

An exponent tells you how many times to multiply a number by itself.

In one line: an exponent means multiply the base by itself, that many times.

Watch It Solved

3³ = ?

multiply 3 by itself 3 times

3 × 3 × 3

= 27

Watch Out

A common mistake is multiplying the base by the exponent instead — thinking 3³ = 3 × 3 = 9. It actually means 3 × 3 × 3, multiplied three times, not twice.

Why It Matters

Exponents describe explosive growth — populations, interest, viral spread — and are the basis for square roots and scientific notation.

Multiplying Powers with the Same Base

The Question

That same bacteria colony triples every hour for 2 hours (3²), then triples for 3 more hours (3³). What's the total growth factor over all 5 hours combined?

The Idea

When you multiply two powers with the same base, just add the exponents: am × an = am+n.

In one line: same base, multiplying → add the exponents.

Watch It Solved

3² × 3³

same base (3) → add the exponents: 2 + 3 = 5

= 3^5

= 243

Watch Out

A common mistake is multiplying the exponents instead of adding them — thinking 3² × 3³ = 3^6. When the operation is multiplication and the base matches, you ADD the exponents, not multiply them.

Why It Matters

This rule is what makes compound growth over multiple stages easy to combine into one single power.
