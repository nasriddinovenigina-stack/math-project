Proportions

The Question

You're told that 3/4 = x/12. What number does x have to be for these two fractions to be equal?

The Idea

A proportion is a statement that two ratios are equal. To solve for a missing number, cross-multiply: if a/b = c/d, then a × d = b × c.

In one line: cross-multiply, then divide to isolate the unknown.

Watch It Solved

3/4 = x/12

3 × 12 = 4 × x

36 = 4x

x = 9

Watch Out

A common mistake is cross-multiplying the wrong pair — multiply diagonally (top-left × bottom-right, and bottom-left × top-right), not straight across the top row or bottom row.

Why It Matters

Proportions are the algebra behind scaling recipes, resizing images, reading maps, and converting between units — the informal "scale it up" trick from ratios becomes a reliable equation you can always solve.
