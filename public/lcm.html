🔗LCM: Least Common Multiple

The Question

One bus leaves every 4 minutes, another every 6 minutes. They just left together — how many minutes until they leave together again?

The Idea

The LCM of two numbers is the smallest number that both of them divide into evenly. You can find it with: LCM = (a × b) ÷ GCF(a, b).

In one line: multiply the numbers, then divide by their GCF.

Watch It Solved

LCM(4, 6)

GCF(4, 6) = 2

LCM = (4 × 6) ÷ 2

= 12

Watch Out

A common mistake is just multiplying the two numbers (4 × 6 = 24) and stopping there — that's a common multiple, but not the smallest one. Always divide by the GCF to shrink it down.

Why It Matters

The LCM is exactly how you find a common denominator when adding fractions, or figure out when repeating events line up.
