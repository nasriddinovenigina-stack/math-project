GCF: Greatest Common Factor

You have 12 samsa and 18 pastries and want to arrange them on identical trays with none left over, using the biggest tray size possible. How many should go on each tray?

The GCF of two numbers is the largest number that divides evenly into both of them.

In one line: list the factors of both, then pick the biggest one they share.

GCF(12, 18)

factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

largest shared factor → 6

GCF(12, 18) = 6

A common mistake is picking a shared factor that isn't the largest — 12 and 18 also share 2 and 3, but the GCF is the biggest one they share, 6.

The GCF is what lets you simplify fractions and split things into the fewest, most even groups.
