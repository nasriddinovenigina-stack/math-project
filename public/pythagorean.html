Pythagorean Theorem

A ladder leans against a wall. Its base is 3 meters from the wall, and it reaches 4 meters up the wall. How long is the ladder?

For a right triangle with legs a and b and hypotenuse c: a² + b² = c².

In one line: square both legs, add them, then take the square root.

legs 3 and 4

3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25

c = √25

c = 5

A common mistake is adding the legs first and then squaring — (3 + 4)² = 49, not 25. You have to square each leg separately before adding.

This is how carpenters check a corner is truly square, and how GPS and screen sizes calculate straight-line distance.
