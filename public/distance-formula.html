🗺️Distance Formula

The Question

Two delivery points on a map are at (1, 2) and (4, 6). How far apart are they, in a straight line?

The Idea

The distance formula comes from the Pythagorean theorem: d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²).

In one line: find the difference in x, the difference in y, square both, add, then take the square root.

Watch It Solved

(1, 2) and (4, 6)

difference in x: 4 − 1 = 3; difference in y: 6 − 2 = 4

d = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16)

d = √25 = 5

Watch Out

A common mistake is adding the x's and y's directly instead of subtracting them first — the formula needs the difference between each pair of coordinates, not their sum.

Why It Matters

The distance formula is how you measure straight-line distance on a map, a coordinate plane, or between two GPS points.
