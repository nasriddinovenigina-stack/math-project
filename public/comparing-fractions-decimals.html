🆚Comparing Fractions & Decimals

The Question

Which is more pizza: 35 of a pizza, or 0.5 of a pizza?

The Idea

To compare a fraction and a decimal, convert them to the same form — turn the fraction into a decimal (divide top by bottom) — then compare directly.

In one line: convert both to the same form (decimals are easiest), then compare.

Watch It Solved

Compare 35 and 0.5

3 ÷ 5 = 0.6

0.6 vs 0.5

0.6 > 0.5, so 35 is more

Watch Out

A common mistake is comparing the numerator or denominator directly without converting — 35 isn't automatically bigger just because 3 is bigger than 0.5's leading digit. Always convert to the same form first.

Why It Matters

Comparing fractions and decimals lets you judge which price, measurement, or score is actually bigger, even when they're written differently.

Enter >, <, or = to show which is bigger (or if they're equal).
