🛹Parabola: Finding the Vertex

The Question

A skate ramp's cross-section follows the curve y = x² − 4x + 1. At what x-position is the very bottom of the ramp?

The Idea

A parabola is the curved graph of y = ax² + bx + c. Its highest or lowest point, the vertex, has an x-value you can find with a simple formula.

In one line: the vertex sits at x = −b ÷ (2a).

Watch It Solved

y = x² − 4x + 1

x = −b ÷ (2 × a) → −(−4) ÷ (2 × 1)

x = 4 ÷ 2

x = 2

Watch Out

A common mistake is forgetting the negative sign on b, or dividing by just "a" instead of "2a" — both throw off the answer. Write out −b ÷ (2a) fully before plugging in numbers.

Why It Matters

The vertex tells you the highest or lowest point of anything that follows a curved path — a thrown object, a satellite dish, a suspension bridge cable.

Enter just the x-coordinate of the vertex (a whole number).
