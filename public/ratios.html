Ratios & Proportions

3 apples cost 15,000 so'm. If the price stays proportional, how much do 5 apples cost?

A ratio compares two quantities. When two ratios are equal, that's a proportion — and you can find a missing value by cross-multiplying.

In one line: cross-multiply, then divide to find the missing number.

3 apples → 15,000 so'm

5 apples → x so'm

write it as a proportion — apples over so'm on both sides

3/15,000 = 5/x

cross-multiply: 3 × x = 15,000 × 5

3x = 75,000

x = 75,000 ÷ 3

x = 25,000 so'm

A common mistake is cross-multiplying the wrong pairs, mixing up which numbers go on top and bottom. Keep matching units in the same position in both ratios — apples with apples, so'm with so'm.

Ratios and proportions show up everywhere — recipes, maps, exchange rates, and scaling designs up or down.
