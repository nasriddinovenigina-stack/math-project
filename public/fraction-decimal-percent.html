🔄Converting Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

The Question

A survey says 34 of students prefer summer. What is that as a decimal, and as a percentage?

The Idea

These are three ways to write the same value: divide the fraction to get a decimal, then multiply the decimal by 100 to get a percentage.

In one line: fraction → decimal by dividing, decimal → percent by ×100.

Watch It Solved

34

3 ÷ 4 = 0.75

0.75 × 100 = 75

34 = 0.75 = 75%

Watch Out

A common mistake is forgetting to move the decimal point when converting to a percentage — writing 0.75 as 0.75% instead of 75%. Multiply by 100 (move the decimal two places right) to go from decimal to percent.

Why It Matters

These three forms show up interchangeably everywhere — recipes use fractions, calculators use decimals, and stores use percentages for discounts.
