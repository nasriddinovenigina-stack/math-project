Multi-Step Equations: Distributive Property

The Question

You and 2 friends order the same combo meal. The restaurant takes the same 3,000 so'm discount off each of your 3 bills, and the discounted bills add up to 24,000 so'm. If each original bill was x so'm, that's 3(x − 3,000) = 24,000. What was the original price of one meal?

The Idea

First distribute (multiply the number outside the parentheses by everything inside), then solve like a regular equation: move numbers across the equals sign (flipping the sign), then divide.

In one line: distribute first, then solve as usual.

Watch It Solved

3(x − 3,000) = 24,000

distribute the 3: 3 × x and 3 × (−3,000)

3x − 9,000 = 24,000

move −9,000 to the other side → it becomes +9,000

3x = 24,000 + 9,000

3x = 33,000

divide both sides by 3

x = 11,000 (so'm)

Watch Out

The most common mistake is forgetting to multiply the number OUTSIDE the parentheses by every term INSIDE — for example, only multiplying the x and forgetting the −3,000 also gets multiplied by 3.

Why It Matters

Distributing first is what lets you solve equations that come from real situations like splitting a discounted total evenly, or scaling a recipe with an adjustment.
