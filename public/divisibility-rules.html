🔍Divisibility Rules

The Question

You want to know if 471 candies can be split evenly among 3 people — without actually dividing. Is there a shortcut?

The Idea

Divisibility rules let you check if a number divides evenly just by looking at its digits: divisible by 2 if it ends in an even digit, by 5 if it ends in 0 or 5, by 10 if it ends in 0, and by 3 (or 9) if the sum of all its digits is divisible by 3 (or 9).

In one line: check the last digit for 2, 5, 10 — check the digit sum for 3 and 9.

Watch It Solved

Is 471 divisible by 3?

add the digits: 4 + 7 + 1 = 12

12 is divisible by 3

yes, 471 is divisible by 3

Watch Out

A common mistake is checking divisibility by 3 using only the last digit — that shortcut only works for 2, 5, and 10. For 3 and 9, you must add up ALL the digits first.

Why It Matters

Divisibility rules let you simplify fractions, check answers, and split things evenly, fast — without a calculator.
