Circles: Area & Circumference

A circular garden has a radius of 5 meters. How much fencing do you need to go all the way around it?

A circle's circumference (distance around) is C = 2πr, and its area is A = πr², where r is the radius. Use π ≈ 3.14.

In one line: circumference = 2πr, area = πr².

radius = 5 meters

C = 2 × π × 5

use π ≈ 3.14

C = 2 × 3.14 × 5

C ≈ 31.4 meters

A common mistake is mixing up radius and diameter — if you're given the diameter, divide by 2 to get the radius first. Also don't confuse the two formulas: circumference uses just r, but area uses r² (r multiplied by itself).

Circle formulas show up in wheels, pipes, clocks, and pizza sizes — anywhere something is round.

Use π ≈ 3.14 and round your answer to 1 decimal place.
