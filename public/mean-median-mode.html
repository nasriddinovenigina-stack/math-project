📈Mean, Median & Mode

The Question

Your test scores this month are 80, 90, 70, 90, 85. What's the mean, median, and mode?

The Idea

Mean is the average (add them up, divide by how many). Median is the middle value when they're sorted in order. Mode is the value that appears most often.

In one line: mean = add & divide, median = sort & find the middle, mode = the most frequent value.

Watch It Solved

Scores: 80, 90, 70, 90, 85

mean: add them all, divide by 5

(80+90+70+90+85) ÷ 5 = 415 ÷ 5

mean = 83

median: sort the scores → 70, 80, 85, 90, 90 → pick the middle one

median = 85

mode: which score appears most often?

mode = 90

Watch Out

A common mistake with median is forgetting to sort the numbers first — the middle of the ORIGINAL list isn't the median unless it's already in order. Always sort from smallest to largest first.

Why It Matters

These three numbers summarize a whole data set in different ways — mean is best for typical size, median resists being skewed by extreme outliers, and mode shows what's most common.
