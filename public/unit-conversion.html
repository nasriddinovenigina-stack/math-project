📏Unit Conversion

The Question

A recipe calls for 750 milliliters of milk, but your measuring jug is marked in liters. How many liters is that?

The Idea

To convert between units, multiply or divide by the same conversion factor every time — like 1000 for milliliters ↔ liters. Moving to a smaller unit means multiplying; moving to a bigger unit means dividing.

In one line: bigger unit → divide, smaller unit → multiply, by the conversion factor.

Watch It Solved

750 ml → liters

1000 ml = 1 l, so divide by 1000

750 ÷ 1000

= 0.75 l

Watch Out

A common mistake is multiplying instead of dividing when converting to a bigger unit — turning 750 ml into 750,000 l instead of 0.75 l. Going to a bigger unit always makes the number smaller.

Why It Matters

Unit conversion is needed anywhere measurements come in different units — cooking, shopping, travel, science.
