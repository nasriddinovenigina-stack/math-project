Slope of a Line

Slope measures how steep a line is — "rise over run." Given two points, slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁).

Points (1, 2) and (4, 11)

slope = (11 − 2) ÷ (4 − 1)

9 ÷ 3

= 3
