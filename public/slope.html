Slope of a Line

A hiking trail is at 2 meters of elevation at the 1 km mark, and by the 4 km mark it's at 11 meters. How steep is the trail, on average?

Slope measures how steep a line is — "rise over run." Given two points, slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁).

In one line: rise over run — change in y divided by change in x.

Points (1, 2) and (4, 11)

slope = (11 − 2) ÷ (4 − 1)

9 ÷ 3

= 3

A common mistake is subtracting the coordinates in mismatched order — doing (11 − 2) for y but then (1 − 4) instead of (4 − 1) for x. Always subtract in the same order for both x and y.

Slope measures rate of change — speed, steepness, growth — one of the most useful ideas in math and science.
