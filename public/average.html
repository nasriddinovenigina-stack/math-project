📊Average (Mean)

The Question

You scored 4, 8, and 6 points in three mini-games at a fair. What's your average score?

The Idea

To find the average, add up all the numbers, then divide by how many numbers there are.

In one line: add them all up, divide by how many there are.

Watch It Solved

4, 8, 6

add them: 4 + 8 + 6 = 18

18 ÷ 3

= 6

Watch Out

A common mistake is dividing by the wrong count — forgetting one of the numbers, or dividing by 2 instead of 3. Always count exactly how many numbers you added.

Why It Matters

Averages summarize a whole set of numbers with one — test scores, prices, ratings, sports stats.
