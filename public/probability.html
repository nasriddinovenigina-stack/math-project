🎲Probability

The Question

A bag has 3 red marbles and 2 blue marbles. If you pick one without looking, what's the probability it's red?

The Idea

Probability measures how likely something is, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). P(event) = number of favorable outcomes ÷ total number of outcomes.

In one line: probability = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes.

Watch It Solved

3 red marbles, 2 blue marbles → 5 total

P(red) = favorable ÷ total

P(red) = 3 ÷ 5

P(red) = 0.6 (or 3/5, or 60%)

Watch Out

A common mistake is dividing by the number of OTHER outcomes instead of the TOTAL — for example, writing 3/2 (red vs blue) instead of 3/5 (red out of everything). Always divide by the total number of possible outcomes, not just the other category.

Why It Matters

Probability is behind weather forecasts, game odds, insurance, and predicting outcomes in everyday decisions.
